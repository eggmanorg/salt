Buttered Leeks
with Thyme

Serves 4  ·  10 minutes preparation  ·  25 minutes cooking

Leeks cooked slowly in butter until they collapse into something closer to a sauce than a vegetable. They will take any amount of patience you give them, and they reheat better than almost anything else on this page.

Ingredients

For the leeks

  • 6 medium leeks, about 900 g
  • 80 g unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 sprigs thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 150 ml dry white wine
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • Black pepper

To finish

  • 2 tbsp double cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 30 g Parmesan, finely grated

Method

  1. Trim the leeks, keeping the pale green. Split them lengthways, rinse thoroughly under cold water, and cut into 2 cm half-moons.
  2. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a wide, heavy pan over a low heat. Add the thyme and the bay leaf and let them sizzle for a minute.
  3. Add the leeks and the salt. Stir to coat, then cover and cook gently for 15 minutes, stirring every few minutes. They should soften without colouring.
  4. Pour in the wine, raise the heat and let it bubble away almost completely, about 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and cook for a further 3 minutes, until the leeks are glossy and thick.
  6. Take off the heat. Remove the thyme and bay. Add the lemon juice, most of the Parmesan and a good grind of pepper. Taste, and add more salt if it needs it.
  7. Serve warm, with the last of the Parmesan scattered over.
